## Herbier vivant

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual design moodboard for an existing low-poly 3D behavioral simulation game, IA_Life; concept preview, not final game art.
Primary request: Direction A, 'Herbier vivant' — a calm naturalist field-journal aesthetic. Create a polished landscape moodboard with a large central in-game scene and smaller orderly material, palette, character and HUD studies around it.
Scene/backdrop: gently rolling grassy walled field with low-poly trees, rocks, blackberry bushes with black berries, four simple humanoid simulation agents; the scene feels observable and uncluttered.
Style/medium: art direction board, stylized low-poly 3D game render combined with clean UI study and material swatches; warm paper and botanical illustration cues without becoming a medieval fantasy game.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape board; central wide three-quarter view of game world; four separate, equally prominent agent swatches/figures in muted vermilion red, sky blue, leaf green, and ochre yellow; a small HUD study with hunger bar, death state, danger marker; organized whitespace.
Lighting/mood: clear late-afternoon daylight, welcoming, readable.
Color palette: warm ivory #F4EEDD, deep pine #263D30, meadow #6F8D5E, blackberry #282033; four agents #C65A45, #4D86B7, #5C9A67, #D9AA3C; hunger amber #D58532, death slate #727B78, danger vivid coral #C94E43.
Materials/textures: paper grain in UI only, matte foliage, warm stone, understated clean shapes.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Show the four agents clearly distinct and status signals based on symbol and shape as well as color. This is a moodboard, not a finished screenshot. Keep visual design feasible for Godot procedural scene and UI.
```

## Observatoire

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual design moodboard for an existing low-poly 3D behavioral simulation game, IA_Life; concept preview.
Primary request: Direction B, 'Observatoire' — cool analytical field-laboratory aesthetic where the 3D world is a clearly readable experimental space. Produce a polished landscape moodboard: one large central in-game view with smaller coordinated UI, color, character, and status studies.
Scene/backdrop: gently rolling grassy rectangular simulation arena bounded by simple walls, sparse low-poly trees, rocks and blackberry bushes, four simple humanoid agents. No technology props in the world; the scientific visual identity lives in the observation HUD.
Style/medium: understated low-poly 3D scene, crisp flat data UI, subtle grid, precise lines, frosted translucent dark panels; publication-quality game art direction board.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape; large three-quarter world view; four separate equally prominent simple agent figures in vivid red #D75B58, blue #4698D2, green #65B977, yellow #E4BE4B. Include a clean close-up of four matching marker shapes, a schematic agent monitoring HUD with hunger meters and inventory icons, a danger zone visual study and muted death state study. Clear generous spacing.
Lighting/mood: neutral overcast daylight with cool sky, sober and highly readable.
Color palette: midnight navy #101D2B for panels, near-white #EFF4F2 text, cool slate #647C87, pale cyan #A9D8DE; agent colors as specified; hunger amber #E5A543, danger orange-red #EB694F, death neutral gray #808B96.
Materials/textures: matte stone, simple foliage, very restrained wireframe and subtle measurement-grid overlays only in UI.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Keep the four agent colors corresponding to red blue green yellow. Avoid futuristic gadgets, robots, glowing neon, fantasy scenery. Status must also communicate with icon silhouettes and patterns, not only color. Moodboard, not final screenshot.
```

## Crépuscule lumineux

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual design moodboard for IA_Life, an existing low-poly 3D behavioral simulation game; concept preview.
Primary request: Direction C, 'Crépuscule lumineux' — poetic twilight forest style, quietly atmospheric yet suitable for tracking four agents and danger states. Make a polished landscape moodboard with a large central in-game scene plus disciplined smaller palette, character, HUD and status studies.
Scene/backdrop: existing type of gently rolling grass arena bounded by low simple walls; sparse stylized trees, rocks and blackberry bushes with visible black berries; four simple humanoid agents. Dusk sky, warm horizon, cool foreground. No magic creatures or new gameplay objects.
Style/medium: restrained stylized low-poly 3D, elegant game art direction board with softly glowing edge light and a dark translucent UI; no photorealism.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape. Large wide world scene; four clearly separated full-body agents in warm red #D66B64, clean blue #62B7D7, mint green #7FC596 and luminous yellow #E8C85A. Include color and material studies, four marker silhouettes, hunger indicator example, a subdued death study, and a danger ring study. Do not obscure the playable space with the UI.
Lighting/mood: blue-violet twilight with warm peach rim light; reflective and calm but readable.
Color palette: deep midnight blue #142232, twilight blue #304765, muted violet #756989, moonlit cream #EDE8DB, blackberry #201C2D; four agents as specified; hunger amber #F2AD54, death cool gray #7A8990, danger saturated red-orange #F0644C.
Materials/textures: matte faceted foliage and stone, subtle luminous accents on markers, restrained UI glass panels.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Maintain clear color separation among red blue green yellow agents, and distinguish hunger/death/danger through symbols and shapes in addition to color. Avoid horror mood, excessive bloom or fog, fantasy props, cyberpunk. Concept board, not final screenshot.
```
